# Three tracks (pick one; sized for one session; "impressive" is not the goal — WORKING is)

**A · The personal instrument.** Replace `my_tool` with something only you
would build: your course deadlines from a dict; a quote-roulette from notes;
your gym's hours; a coin-flip you've rigged with reasons. Smallest honest
win — the model reaches a fact of YOUR life it could never know.

**B · The dataset liberator** (the Whitney pattern). Take a small public
dataset (a CSV of anything you care about), load it in the server, expose
`search(query)` + `get(id)`. The lesson from the museum: a dataset nobody
can converse with might as well not exist; twenty lines make it exist.

**C · The form that fills itself** (MCP apps, adventurous). A tool whose
argument schema IS a small form (name, choice, date) — Claude collects the
fields conversationally and your server acts on the structured result.
Closest to how real course instruments (polls, sign-ups) get built.
